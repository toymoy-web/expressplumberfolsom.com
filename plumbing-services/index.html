What we handle

All plumbing services in Folsom, CA

From a same-day drain clog to a full repipe, here's the complete list of what we handle.

Emergency plumbing is handled directly on our homepage, call any hour for an active leak, burst pipe, or urgent repair. Every other service we offer is listed below with its own dedicated page covering what to expect, common signs you need it, and answers to the questions we hear most.

Services are grouped loosely by how they usually come up. Water heater work covers installation, tankless service, and repair. Drain and sewer work covers cleaning, hydro jetting, and full sewer line repair. Leak-related work covers detection, slab leaks, repiping, and burst pipes. Fixture work covers toilets, faucets, sinks, and garbage disposals, and specialty categories round out septic pumping, gas lines, water treatment, and commercial plumbing.

A few of these pages exist because of what our call history actually shows rather than a generic industry list. Leak detection and slab leak repair, for instance, gets a dedicated page because it's one of the most common reasons Folsom homeowners call, not because every plumbing company's website happens to include it. The same goes for water heater repair versus full replacement, we split those into separate pages because the decision between the two is a genuinely different conversation depending on your unit's age and condition.

Two pages on this list, water damage restoration and water mitigation, are a distinct service category from plumbing repair itself, cleanup, drying, and structural repair after water damage rather than fixing the pipe that caused it. Both use a separate phone number, shown on those two pages specifically, since that side of the business is coordinated through a dedicated response team rather than our standard plumbing dispatch, and keeping the numbers separate keeps call routing accurate on both sides.

If you're not sure which page matches what you're dealing with, that's a completely normal starting point, call (714) 750-8637 and describe what you're noticing rather than trying to self-diagnose from a list of page titles. We'll point you to the right service, or in many cases just get a technician on the way rather than making you spend time researching it yourself first.

Every page listed here reflects a service we actually perform regularly, not a padded list assembled to look comprehensive. If you call about something that isn't listed, ask anyway. Plumbing work doesn't always fit neatly into a fixed set of categories, and we'd rather have a direct conversation about your specific situation than turn away a call because it doesn't match a page title exactly.

What to do if you have an active leak or water damage right now

  • Shut off your main water valve if it's safe to reach
  • Move valuables and electronics away from the water
  • For an active plumbing emergency, call (714) 750-8637
  • For water damage cleanup already underway, visit our water mitigation page for that team's dedicated line
