About us

A local crew in Folsom, CA, not a franchise call center

Folsom Express Plumbing is a licensed plumbing business serving Folsom, CA and the surrounding communities.

We're a plumbing business based in Folsom, California, operating under California's CSLB C-36 plumbing classification. We work throughout Folsom and dispatch to the surrounding communities covered on our service area pages, from El Dorado Hills to Orangevale.

Our approach is straightforward: diagnose honestly before recommending a fix, explain pricing clearly before starting work, and carry the common parts needed to finish most jobs in a single visit. We don't upsell a bigger job than a house actually needs, and when a repair turns out to need a specialist outside our scope, water damage restoration after a leak, for instance, we say so directly rather than stretching our own scope to cover it.

Every technician we send is trained to recognize the difference between Folsom's older Historic District homes, with original galvanized supply lines and drain systems, and the newer slab-foundation construction common in neighborhoods like Broadstone and Empire Ranch. That distinction changes what we check first and what we bring on the truck, and it's part of why we don't treat every call the same way a generic dispatch service would treat it.

We're licensed and insured, and all work is performed to California Plumbing Code (UPC-based) standards, with permits pulled through the City of Folsom where required. If you have questions about our licensing, insurance, or a specific job before booking, call us directly and we'll answer honestly.

Real customer reviews will be added here once we have verified ones to share. We won't publish invented testimonials or made-up star ratings in the meantime, if you don't see reviews on this site yet, that's why.

We're also straightforward about what we don't do: we don't offer roofing, electrical, or general contracting services. If a job you call about turns out to need one of those trades instead of a plumber, we'll tell you plainly rather than taking on work outside our actual expertise. Staying focused on plumbing is part of how we keep the quality consistent across every job we do take.

We're a small, local operation rather than a franchise, which means the person answering the phone actually knows the crew that will show up, not a call center reading from a script with no visibility into who's actually available or how far away they are. When you call, we can usually give you a real estimate of arrival time based on where our nearest technician actually is, not a generic same-day promise designed to get you off the phone.

Pricing is discussed before any work begins, not after. For non-emergency work, we provide a free estimate. For emergency calls, we explain the emergency service structure and any applicable rates before dispatching, so you're never surprised by an invoice after the fact. If a job's scope changes once we're on-site, revealing a bigger problem than the original call described, we stop and explain the new scope and cost before continuing rather than running up a bill silently.

We keep detailed records of the work we perform at each property, which matters if you ever need documentation for an insurance claim, a home sale disclosure, or simply want to know what was done and when the next time something comes up. Ask for a copy of your service history any time, we're happy to provide it.

Our service area extends beyond Folsom itself to the surrounding communities detailed on our service area page, including El Dorado Hills, Rancho Cordova, Granite Bay, Orangevale, and Gold River. Each of those pages covers the specific plumbing patterns we see in that community, since a house in Historic Folsom and a house in newer Broadstone construction don't fail the same way, and neither do homes in the surrounding towns we serve.

If you're comparing us against a national chain or a franchise operation, the practical difference usually comes down to who actually shows up. A franchise dispatches whoever's available from a regional pool, sometimes a subcontractor you've never spoken with before, with no particular knowledge of Folsom's housing stock. We're a fixed, local team, and the person diagnosing your issue is the same kind of technician who diagnosed the last call in your neighborhood last week, not a stranger dispatched from across the county.

We answer our own phones during business hours and route emergency calls to whichever technician is genuinely closest, rather than working through a national scheduling queue that doesn't account for actual drive time. If you've had a frustrating experience with a bigger provider before, slow callbacks, vague arrival windows, a different technician every visit, that's specifically the gap we're built to close for Folsom and the surrounding communities.
