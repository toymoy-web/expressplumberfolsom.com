A lot of plumbing calls don't start with a clear diagnosis. They start with a general sense that something's off. It might be a sound that wasn't there before, a slightly damp spot on the ceiling, or water pressure that feels different than it used to. Sometimes a fixture is just acting strange in a way that's hard to describe over the phone. That's completely normal, and it's exactly the kind of call we're set up to handle, we send someone who can actually look at the whole picture rather than requiring you to self-diagnose before calling.

Some of the most common general repair calls turn out to be smaller than people expect: a running toilet, a slow drain, a dripping faucet, a valve that's stuck or won't fully shut off. Others turn out to be bigger than the original symptom suggested, a small ceiling stain that's actually a slow leak that's been running inside a wall for weeks. Either way, the value of a general repair visit is in the honest diagnosis, we tell you what we actually find, not just what would be most profitable to fix.

For older Folsom homes, particularly in the Historic District, general repair calls often trace back to original plumbing that's simply reached the end of its practical life. Aging galvanized supply lines, original fixtures with decades of wear, or a drain system sized for decades-old usage patterns rather than how the home is used today are all common culprits. For newer homes, the more common pattern is an installation shortcut from original construction finally showing itself, a fitting that was never quite tightened correctly, or a component that was undersized for the home's actual demand.

If a repair turns out to be part of a bigger pattern rather than an isolated issue, recurring leaks pointing toward a repiping need, or a drain issue tracing back to a failing sewer lateral, we'll tell you clearly. We'd rather point you to the service that actually addresses it than repeatedly patch the same symptom on separate visits. Our leak detection and sewer line repair pages cover what those bigger jobs actually involve.

We carry a broad range of common parts and fittings on every truck specifically so a general repair call doesn't turn into two visits for something straightforward. Every repair is performed to California Plumbing Code (UPC-based) standards regardless of how minor the fix seems at first glance.

One pattern is worth mentioning. A newer home with a plumbing issue that seems to come out of nowhere sometimes traces back to a single shortcut taken during original construction. Examples include a fitting hand-tightened instead of properly sealed, or a support strap left out on a long pipe run that eventually sags and stresses a joint. Another is a vent line that technically passed inspection but creates a slow, recurring drainage issue years later. These aren't things you'd catch without opening a wall, which is exactly why an honest diagnosis matters more than guessing based on the house's age alone.

Leak repairs and pipe leaks

Many people call without knowing exactly where the leak is. They see a stain, hear a drip, or find a damp cabinet floor. We start with where and when it leaks, check the likely sources, and give you a clear diagnosis.

Some leaks are simple and some are the first sign of a bigger problem. A downstairs leak, for instance, may start with an upstairs bathroom. We say which one yours looks like.

Low water pressure and no water at the house

Low pressure can come from a failing pressure regulator, a partly closed valve, a clogged aerator, corroded pipe, or a leak. Callers also describe having no water at all. We check at the fixture first, then at the main valve, the regulator, and the meter.

If the issue is on the City side of the meter, we will say so and give you the right number to call.

Installs, new construction, and remodel plumbing

We also handle installations of tubs, showers, and sink hookups, and rough plumbing for additions and new construction. Tell us what you are building and we will explain the work involved.

Common questions

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Active leaks you cannot stop, sewage backing up, no water at all, a gas smell, and a broken water heater that is flooding are all emergencies. If water is damaging your home right now, call.

Do I need to know exactly what's wrong before calling?

No. Describe what you're noticing, a sound, a smell, a change in pressure, and we'll diagnose the actual cause on-site rather than requiring a self-diagnosis first.

What if my problem turns out to be bigger than expected?

We'll tell you honestly and explain the real scope before doing any additional work, rather than surprising you with an expanded job partway through.

Do you carry parts on the truck for common repairs?

Yes, we stock a broad range of common fittings and parts specifically so most general repairs are done in a single visit.

Can you give a second opinion on a repair another plumber recommended?

Yes, we're happy to assess and give an honest opinion, including if we think the original recommendation was accurate.

Can you check why I have no water or low pressure?

Yes. We check the fixture, the main valve, the pressure regulator, and the meter. If the problem is on the City side, we tell you and give you the number to call.
