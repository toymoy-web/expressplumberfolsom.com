Sewer Line Repair in Folsom, CA

A sewer line that keeps backing up usually has one real cause. We camera-inspect first so you're paying for the actual fix, not a guess.

A failing sewer line rarely announces itself as a single dramatic event. More often it's a pattern: a main line clog that comes back every few months, gurgling drains, or a slow-draining tub that seemed unrelated until it happened again. By the time raw sewage is actually backing up into the house, the underlying problem has usually been developing for a while. That's why we push for a camera inspection at the first sign of a repeat pattern, rather than waiting for the worst-case call.

In Folsom specifically, the age of the sewer lateral matters a lot. Homes in the Historic District and other older parts of the city were often built with clay pipe, and clay sewer laterals develop joint separation and root intrusion as they age, sometimes decades before the rest of the house shows any wear. Newer construction in areas like Empire Ranch and Natoma Station typically has PVC laterals, which are more resistant to root intrusion but not immune to bellies (low spots where the pipe has settled) if the original grading or backfill wasn't done well.

A sewer camera inspection is the single most useful diagnostic tool for this. It shows us exactly where a blockage, crack, or root mass is located along the line, and how far it is from the cleanout, information we simply can't get by guessing based on symptoms alone. That location and severity is what determines whether the right fix is a localized spot repair, a full trenchless replacement, or in some cases just a hydro jetting and root-cutting pass if the pipe itself is still structurally sound.

Trenchless repair, including pipe lining and lateral replacement without a full trench dig, is worth asking about specifically if your yard has established landscaping or hardscape you don't want torn up. It's not right for every situation (a severely collapsed section usually still needs traditional excavation), but where it applies it can mean the difference between a weekend without landscaping and weeks of yard restoration afterward.

If a sewer backup has already caused water damage or contamination inside the house, that cleanup and restoration is a distinct service from the sewer repair itself, water damage and mold remediation work is handled separately from fixing the pipe that caused it. We'll be clear about which part of the job is the plumbing fix and which part needs a restoration specialist, rather than blurring the two.

If what actually brought you here is a single slow fixture rather than a repeat main-line pattern, our drain cleaning and unclogging page is the more direct starting point, since not every clog points to a failing sewer lateral. We'll tell you plainly and honestly which situation actually applies once we've looked at the line ourselves. Any lateral replacement is installed to California Plumbing Code (UPC-based) standards and permitted through the City of Folsom.

When a sewer camera inspection is worth it

  • Main line clogs that recur every few months
  • Home has original clay sewer lateral (common in older Folsom homes)
  • Sewage odor in the yard, especially near the cleanout or a low spot
  • Slow drains in multiple fixtures with no other clear cause
  • Selling or buying a home built before the 1990s

Before you call

Common questions

How do I know if my sewer lateral is clay or PVC?

Age and neighborhood are a starting clue, older homes in Historic Folsom are more likely to have clay, newer construction typically has PVC, but a camera inspection confirms it and shows condition, not just material.

Is trenchless sewer repair right for my yard?

Often, if the pipe damage is localized rather than a full collapse. It avoids tearing up established landscaping, but we confirm it's appropriate with a camera inspection before recommending it over traditional excavation.

What does a sewer camera inspection cost?

We'll quote it based on line length and access when you call. It's usually far less than the cost of guessing wrong on a repair approach.

Can tree roots really crack a sewer pipe?

Roots more often exploit an existing joint gap or hairline crack than cause the initial damage, but once inside they widen the opening and worsen a backup pattern over time.
