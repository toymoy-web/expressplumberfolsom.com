Burst Pipe Repair in Folsom, CA

A burst pipe doesn't wait for business hours. We answer, walk you through shutting off the water, and get a crew moving before the damage spreads.

The first thing that matters with a burst pipe is speed, both ours and yours. If you're standing in front of an active leak right now, find your home's main shutoff valve, usually near where the water line enters the house or at the street-side meter box, and turn it off before anything else. That single step limits damage far more than waiting for us to arrive, and we'll walk you through finding it over the phone if you're not sure where it is.

Folsom doesn't see the hard freezes that cause most burst pipes in colder climates, but that doesn't make the area immune. The more common local cause is a sudden pressure surge, often from a water hammer event when a fast-closing valve or appliance stops water flow abruptly, hitting an aging or already-weakened section of pipe hard enough to split it. Galvanized pipe that's already thinned from internal corrosion is especially vulnerable to this kind of surge failure, which ties back to why we flag pipe age as a real factor during any service call, not just repipe consultations.

Occasional winter cold snaps do happen in Sacramento County. Exposed pipe runs, an outdoor hose bib, or a line in an uninsulated crawlspace or garage, can freeze and crack even in a generally mild climate if the exposure is direct enough on a cold enough night. If you've had a pipe freeze once in a particular spot, it's a real candidate for insulation before it happens again, since that location clearly isn't protected the way the rest of the house's plumbing is.

Repairing the burst itself is usually straightforward once we've located it, a section of damaged pipe is cut out and replaced, or in some cases a repair coupling is used for a smaller crack, always to California Plumbing Code (UPC-based) material and joining standards. The bigger question is often whether the burst was an isolated event or a symptom of a broader problem. If the same section of pipe has failed more than once, or if it's part of an aging galvanized run, that's worth a conversation about our repiping service rather than treating each burst as unrelated.

If water has already spread to flooring or drywall, or has been running for a while before it was caught, the water damage cleanup itself is a distinct job from the pipe repair. Drying, moisture remediation, and preventing mold growth is specialized restoration work, not something the same plumbing repair visit typically covers. We'll tell you clearly when that's the case so the right specialist gets involved quickly, rather than waiting on us to figure out something outside our scope.

What to do the moment you find a burst pipe

  • Shut off the main water valve immediately, don't wait for us to arrive
  • Turn off electricity to the affected area if water is near outlets or fixtures
  • Move valuables and furniture away from the water if it's safe to do so
  • Take photos for insurance before cleanup begins
  • Call us with the location and severity so we can prioritize dispatch

Before you call

Common questions

Where is my home's main water shutoff valve?

Usually near where the water line enters the house (often in a garage, basement, or exterior wall) or at the street-side meter box. We'll help you locate it over the phone if needed.

Why did a pipe burst if Folsom doesn't really freeze?

Most local bursts come from a pressure surge (water hammer) hitting an aging or weakened section of pipe, not freezing. Occasional cold snaps can still crack exposed pipe in uninsulated spots.

Is a burst pipe always an emergency?

Yes, treat it as one. Even a small crack can release a large volume of water quickly once pressure is behind it. Shut off the main valve and call us right away.

Will insurance cover a burst pipe repair?

Often the resulting water damage is covered, though coverage varies by policy. We can provide documentation of the repair for your claim.
