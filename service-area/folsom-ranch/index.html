New homes still need a plumber you can call. Here is how to protect your warranty and handle early issues.

Folsom Ranch lies in the Folsom Plan Area, a 3,520-acre area of the city bounded by Highway 50, White Rock Road, Prairie City Road and the El Dorado County line. The City of Folsom says about 11,000 homes are planned over the next 25 to 30 years. Current neighborhoods include Broadstone Estates, Enclave at Folsom Ranch, Folsom Heights, Mangini Ranch, Russell Ranch and White Rock Springs Ranch.

Because this area is growing, homes here are among the newest in Folsom. Listing sites show Folsom Ranch homes with a median build year around 2022, but those figures are not verified. The important point is that a newer home is not immune to plumbing problems. It is just earlier in its life.

Warranty first, then service

Most new homes come with a builder warranty. Before you pay anyone to open a wall or change a fixture, read your warranty and call the builder about a defect that may be covered. We are glad to look at a problem and document what we find so you can give it to the builder.

Warranty or not, an active leak should be stopped right away. Shut off the water at the fixture or the main valve and call. Keep photos, dates and any invoices, because builders and insurers usually ask for them. Water damage grows quickly, and our burst pipe repair page explains what to do while you wait.

Early issues in newer homes

Common early calls include a toilet that runs, a faucet that drips, a water heater that is too small once the household grows, and drains that slow after landscaping or construction debris. These are usually quick to fix. See our water heater installation and drain cleaning pages.

New neighborhoods also add new water meters and new landscaping on irrigation lines. If your bill is higher than expected, check the meter with all fixtures off, then call us if it keeps moving.

Before you dig or landscape

New homes often get new backyards, and that means trenching for irrigation, fences and patios. Call Underground Service Alert at 1-800-642-2444 before you dig so buried utilities are marked. A cut water or gas line is a far bigger problem than the project that caused it, and our burst pipe repair team is available if one is damaged.

Upgrades homeowners ask about

Many owners add a water softener or filtration system, a gas line for an outdoor kitchen, or a second water heater. Our softener and filtration and gas line pages explain the options. Ask us before you buy, because the right choice depends on your home and your goals.

Common questions

Will calling a plumber void my builder warranty?

That depends on the warranty terms, so read yours first. If a defect may be covered, call the builder before paying for repairs. We can document a problem for you to share with them.

Where exactly is the Folsom Plan Area?

According to the City of Folsom, it is a 3,520-acre area bounded by Highway 50, White Rock Road, Prairie City Road and the El Dorado County line.

Do you install water softeners in new homes?

Yes. Call us to talk through whether a softener or filtration system makes sense for your home.
