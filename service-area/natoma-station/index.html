Natoma Station is in southern Folsom, about three miles from Historic Folsom, near the Folsom Premium Outlets, the Willow Creek Recreation Area and Lake Natoma. Light rail runs along nearby Folsom Boulevard. A guide on Homes.com describes mostly single-family homes, many in a Spanish style with stucco and tile roofs, with a median build year around 1993.

A resident letter to the Folsom City Council says the area was developed in the 1990s. Neither source is an official record of when your house was built, so confirm yours from your own paperwork. What matters for plumbing is simple. A home built in the early 1990s has had decades of use, and parts that wear out are due for a look.

Older fixtures and California's water rules

California has a law on noncompliant plumbing fixtures that applies to property built on or before January 1, 1994. Toilets that use more than 1.6 gallons per flush, showerheads above 2.5 gallons per minute and faucets above 2.2 gallons per minute count as noncompliant, and sellers must disclose that in writing. If your home was built around that date, the rule may apply to you.

Replacing a toilet or a faucet is something we do every week. See our toilet repair and installation and faucet repair pages, or call and ask whether the work fits your sale or your own plans.

Pinhole leaks in copper pipe

The City of Folsom has an investigation page on pinhole leaks in copper pipes. The City says the majority of reports involve cold water lines and invites residents to contact it at 916-461-6177 or pinholeleaks@folsom.ca.us. We do not claim that your home has this problem. We do suggest checking for damp spots, stained drywall or a rising bill and calling quickly if you see them.

A small pinhole leak is a good reason for leak detection before any wall is opened. If a section of pipe keeps failing, repiping may be worth discussing, but only after we have looked at the actual pipe.

Storm season near the lake

The City of Folsom provides sandbags 24 hours a day during rain events at a site behind 50 Natoma Street, with proof of Folsom residency, and residents bring their own shovel. The City also notes that leaves can clog storm drains, so keep the drain near your home clear. If water collects near the house, our sump pump page explains one way to move it away.

Water heaters at replacement age

A water heater that has served since the 1990s or early 2000s is often near the end of its life. Leaks at the base, rusty water or constant relighting are signs to act. Our water heater repair page covers the usual causes.
