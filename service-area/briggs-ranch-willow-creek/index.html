Plumber in Briggs Ranch and Willow Creek, Folsom, CA

Homes from the 1980s are at a mid-life stage where a short inspection can save a bigger repair.

Call (714) 750-8637 Message us

Briggs Ranch is a Folsom neighborhood near Folsom Lake. A guide on Homes.com groups it with nearby subdivisions including Willow Creek Estates and Rancho Diablo, and describes newer traditional homes, split-levels and L-shaped ranch homes, often with attached garages and private pools. The same guide gives a median build year of 1987.

That figure is an aggregate estimate and not a record of your home. If your house is from the 1980s, though, it has been in service for roughly four decades, which is a good moment to review what has been updated and what has not.

A mid-life checklist

We suggest owners look at the age of the water heater, the condition of shutoff valves under sinks and toilets, and the water pressure at the main fixtures. Old shutoff valves that have not been turned in years can seize or leak when finally used. Replacing them is a small job that makes future repairs easier.

If your water heater is more than a decade old, plan for replacement before it fails. Our repair and replacement pages explain the options.

Repiping, only when needed

Some owners of older homes ask whether they need to repipe. We do not recommend it because of age alone. If a pipe keeps leaking or water pressure has dropped across the whole house, we inspect and tell you what we see. Our repiping page explains when it makes sense and what the work involves.

Mature landscaping, sewer lines and digging

Neighborhoods that have been settled for decades usually have established trees and landscaping. The City of Folsom advises residents not to plant trees over the sewer line, and it says the sewer cleanout is most often a 4-inch green circular lid. If you plan to dig for a new fence post, patio or irrigation line, call Underground Service Alert at 1-800-642-2444 first so buried lines are marked.

If several drains slow at once, roots or buildup in the main line are worth ruling out. Our sewer line repair page explains how a camera inspection works, and our drain cleaning page covers clearing a stubborn clog.

Pools, irrigation and the water bill

Private pools and irrigation are common in this area, and a leak in either can quietly raise a bill. Check the meter with all fixtures off. For a leak on the City side of the meter, call the City of Folsom water leak line at 916-461-6177 (weekdays during City office hours) or the after-hours water emergency line at 916-461-6400. For a leak on your side, our leak detection service can find it.

Common questions

Do 1980s homes need repiping?

Not automatically. Age alone is not a reason. If a pipe keeps leaking or pressure has dropped throughout the house, we inspect and explain whether a repair or repipe makes sense.

My shutoff valve under the sink will not turn. What do I do?

Do not force it, because an old valve can break. Call us and we can replace it so you have a working shutoff.
