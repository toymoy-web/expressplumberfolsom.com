Broadstone sits along Highway 50 in southern Folsom, near Iron Point Road, East Bidwell Street, Broadstone Plaza and the Folsom Premium Outlets. A neighborhood guide on Homes.com lists mostly single-family houses plus a few condos, with some homes in gated communities such as Bentley Square and Vessona, and a median build year around 1999.

A public comment to the Folsom City Council describes Broadstone, Natoma Station and Prairie Oaks as developed in the 1990s, though it is a resident letter and not an official record. We use these sources as rough context only. The plumbing in your home depends on the builder, the year and any updates, which is why we inspect before we recommend.

Condos, townhomes and shared walls

Attached homes add a question that single-family houses do not: where did the water come from, and whose line is it? A leak under a shared wall can affect a neighbor, and some repairs involve an HOA. If you live in a condo or a gated community, check your association's rules about shutoffs and repairs before work begins.

We can document what we find in writing so you have something to give your association or insurer. Our leak detection work traces a leak without tearing out more than needed.

Leaks, bills and your meter

If your water bill rises and nothing has changed, check the meter with all fixtures off. For a leak on the City side of the meter, call the City of Folsom water leak line at 916-461-6177 (weekdays during City office hours) or the after-hours water emergency line at 916-461-6400. For a leak after the meter, that is a plumbing repair we can handle.

Common culprits are toilets that keep refilling, irrigation lines and slow drips under sinks. Our toilet repair page explains how to tell a running toilet from a hidden supply leak.

What to have ready when you call

It helps to know where your main shutoff is, what the fixture is doing and when it started. If you live in a condo or a gated community, have your HOA contact and gate instructions handy so a technician is not delayed at the entry. Photos of a stain or a leak are useful to us and to your association.

Water heaters and everyday repairs

Homes from the 1990s and 2000s are now old enough that original water heaters are often at or past replacement age. A heater that leaks at the base, makes popping sounds or runs out of hot water fast should be looked at promptly. See our water heater repair and replacement pages.

Common questions

I live in a Broadstone condo. Who pays for a leak behind the wall?

That depends on your association's rules and where the leak started. Ask your HOA before work begins. We can document what we find so you have it for the association or your insurer.

How do I tell a running toilet from a hidden leak?

Put a few drops of food coloring in the tank and wait without flushing. If color shows in the bowl, the flapper is leaking. If not, check the meter with all fixtures off.

Who do I call for a City water leak?

The City of Folsom's water leak line is 916-461-6177, and the after-hours water emergency line is 916-461-6400. For leaks inside your home or after the meter, call us.
